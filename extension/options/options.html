Opening and closing

Off: while unfamiliar sounds is on, Spotify links open with it; while it is off, they open normally. On: while it is on, each Spotify link opened outside it asks first.

Off: switching unfamiliar sounds off closes its tabs. On: they reopen as normal Spotify tabs.

Descriptions

Hidden descriptions show "???". Shown ones have every name unfamiliar sounds knows swapped for its stand-in; names it hasn't seen yet would show as written.

Playlist titles

Off: every playlist gets a stand-in title. On: Spotify's own playlists that unfamiliar sounds judges to be about a theme or mood ("Rainy Day Jazz") keep their real title. A title can still name an artist unfamiliar sounds has never seen (for example "Mixtape #4 <artist>"), which is why this is off by default.

You

Off: your name and profile picture stay as they are.

Off: your playlists keep their titles and descriptions, and keep any picture you uploaded; only Spotify's automatic covers (album collages and single album covers) are replaced. On: their titles, descriptions and pictures are all replaced, like everyone else's.

Hidden pages

Discographies, "This Is" playlists, charts and similar pages are covered by a short notice with a "Show anyway" button. Off: they are shown (with stand-in names, as everywhere).

Seed

Every stand-in name and picture comes from a seed. By default everyone shares one built-in seed, so everyone sees the same stand-ins. You can use your own instead; the built-in one is never shown.
